Welcome to Nocturne VMs

Before you launch your first machine, please read the guidelines below.

Usage Rules
  • Do not install or distribute malware, viruses, ransomware, keyloggers, or any harmful software.
  • Do not attempt to attack, scan, or exploit other systems from your VM.
  • Do not use VMs for cryptocurrency mining.
  • Do not access, store, or distribute illegal content of any kind.
  • Do not attempt to bypass resource limits or escape the VM environment.
  • Your VM resets when you leave. Don't store anything important inside it.
Technical Specs
  • OS: Debian 13 (Trixie) with XFCE desktop
  • Browser: Firefox ESR (pre-installed)
  • Access: Full root / sudo access
  • Session: Ephemeral, wiped on disconnect
Supported Software

You can install packages from the Debian repos with apt. Common supported software:

FirefoxVS CodeLibreOfficeGIMP VLCPythonNode.jsGCC / G++ GitDockerBlenderAudacity

Do not install software from untrusted sources. Stick to apt, Flatpak, or official project sites.
